# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

WA Privacy Pro ("the extension") is built to protect your privacy, not to collect data.

## What the extension does

The extension applies a visual blur to contact names, message text and media on `https://web.whatsapp.com`. All of this happens locally inside your browser.

## Data we collect

**None.** The extension does not collect, record, sell, or share any personal information, browsing activity, messages, contacts, or media.

## Data stored on your device

The extension saves only your preferences:

| Item | Where it is stored | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Blur switches (`allBlur`, `blurNames`, `blurMessages`, `blurMedia`) | `chrome.storage.sync` | Remember your choices and sync them across your own signed-in Chrome profile. |
| Theme (`light` or `dark`) | Extension `localStorage` | Remember your preferred popup theme. |

These values never leave your browser except through Chrome's own profile sync, which is controlled by your Google account settings.

## Network requests

The extension makes **no network requests** and loads no remote code, analytics, or trackers.

## Permissions

- `storage`: to save the preferences listed above.
- Host access to `https://web.whatsapp.com/*`: to apply blur styles on WhatsApp Web only.

## Third parties

No data is shared with any third party.
